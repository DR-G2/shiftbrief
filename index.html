Before you use ShiftBrief

ShiftBrief is a writing aid for fictitious or already de-identified practice notes. It is not a medical device, not a diagnostic tool, and not a substitute for a clinician’s own handover. You remain responsible for every handover. Do not type real patient names, record numbers, or official chart text.

Short notes are sent to an on-device model in Chrome when one is available. Longer notes, or a note you explicitly send to the cloud, leave the device and are processed by a cloud model through Firebase AI Logic. Recent briefs are stored only in this browser.
